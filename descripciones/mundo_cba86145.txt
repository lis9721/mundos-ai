A dramatic storm threatens Aerópolis, the archipelago of floating islands, as jagged lightning bolts cut a sky of bruised violet and electric blue. The city’s skeletal glass towers and wrought‑iron bridges gleam in a faint golden sheen, while patches of verdant moss cling to the cliff edges. A single, crimson flash illuminates the horizon, casting long, elongated shadows over the misty floating platforms. The composition centers on a towering spire, its silhouette framed by the swirling clouds, and the dynamic contrast between the stark reds and muted greens creates a surreal, cinematic atmosphere in a high‑contrast, hyper‑realistic style.
