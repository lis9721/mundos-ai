An ethereal cityscape at twilight, where spiraling towers of translucent glass gleam in deep violet-purple against a bruised sky, while red banners flutter beside blue waterfalls, and green vines creep across bronze facades, illuminated by gold lanterns that cast a warm halo over cobblestone streets. The composition centers on a floating plaza where a silver statue of a phoenix rises from a crystal pool, reflecting the riot of colors. The lighting is dramatic, with rim lights from a setting sun that accentuates the textures, and the style blends surrealism with high‑contrast oil‑painting depth.
