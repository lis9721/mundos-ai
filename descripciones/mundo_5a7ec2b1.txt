In the boundless sky of Aerópolis, a city of shimmering stone and crystal spires hovers above a sea of cotton clouds, its rooftops and bridges arranged like a lattice of golden and black filaments. At dusk, the sky turns a bruised blue, reflected in the glassy towers that glow with subtle green bioluminescence, while a crimson ribbon of river snakes through the archways, its banks bordered by rusted red arches. The composition frames a dramatic horizon where the city’s spires lean toward a distant sun, casting long shadows that contrast sharply against the soft, luminous clouds.
