In the sky, a sprawling city of Aerópolis rises among billowing clouds, its crystalline spires and arching bridges gleaming in a soft twilight. A winding golden ribbon of skyway threads the floating islands together, reflecting the faint glow of distant suns. Verdant vines cascade from balconies, intertwining with emerald terraces that pulse with life. The city’s rooftops blaze in vivid red, punctuating the horizon, while panels of translucent blue glass catch the last rays of daylight. As dusk deepens, a gentle cascade of deep violet‑purple luminescence filters through the cloud canopy, casting a dreamlike glow over the entire archipelago.
